## Facts (from the paper, each with location: Sec./p./Table/Fig.)
- Task / problem: adapting a supervised pre-trained 3D CT organ-segmentation foundation model to a new domain or organ with both few labelled volumes and few trainable parameters; the authors name this setting Few-Shot Efficient Fine-Tuning (FSEFT) (Abstract; Sec. 3.2; Fig. 2).
- Meaning of "few-shot": K fully labelled support volumes per target task, K = {1, 5, 10} ("low data regime"), plus K = 30 as "large data regime"; no validation data during adaptation (Sec. 3.2.1, Eq. 2; Sec. 4.3.1–4.3.2). Each adaptation-evaluation run repeated with three random seeds (Sec. 4.4); the tables report means only, no standard deviations.
- Data (Table 1, p. 7; Sec. 4.1): pre-training on 9 public CT datasets (BTCV, CHAOS, LiTS, KiTS, AbdomenCT-1K, AMOS, MSD subtasks, AbdomenCT-12organ, CT-ORG), 2,042 partially labelled scans, 29 structures. Adaptation on TotalSegmentator (1,024 scans in the dataset, 1,203 listed in Table 1; 9 base organs, binary, one organ at a time; novel: lung lobes, heart and gluteus parcellation = 16 structures) and FLARE'22 (50 labelled training scans, 9 base organs, multi-class). Preprocessing: 1.5 mm isotropic, HU clipped to [−175, 250], scaled to [0, 1] (Sec. 4.1.2).
- Method (two sentences): a Swin-UNETR (48 output features per voxel, encoder initialised from Tang et al. self-supervised weights) is pre-trained with a masked Dice loss over partial labels (Sec. 3.1, Eq. 1; Sec. 4.2). For adaptation the authors compare full fine-tuning, encoder PEFT (BitFit, LoRA with r = 4 on key-query-value, AdaptFormer with rank 16, Affine-LN; for CNNs Bias, Affine-BN, residual 1×1 adapters) with the decoder frozen for base organs, and black-box methods on frozen output features (linear probe, a proposed residual 3D-convolutional "Spatial Adapter"), optionally with transductive inference that penalises predicted organ size deviating by more than margin m = 0.1 from the support-set average (Sec. 3.2.2–3.3, Eq. 3–4; Sec. 4.3.3–4.3.4).
- Setting: single target task per adaptation (one organ, or all nine organs jointly in FLARE'22); a new linear head initialised from the pre-trained class prototype of that organ (Sec. 4.3.2); no text prompts; no sequence of tasks and no continual learning. Catastrophic forgetting appears only as motivation for PEFT (Sec. 1; Sec. 3.2.2); performance on the pre-training tasks after adaptation is not measured.
- Evaluation: DSC per organ on held-out query volumes, threshold 0.5 (binary) or argmax (multi-class) (Sec. 4.4). Baselines: zero-shot pre-trained model, full fine-tuning from self-supervised weights (Tang et al.) and from their own model (Sec. 4.3.5). Also transferred to CLIP-Driven model and SuPreM (Swin-UNETR, 3D U-Net) (Sec. 5.4, Tables 8–9).
- Results, TotalSegmentator base organs, mean DSC over 9 organs (Table 2, p. 10): zero-shot 72.41. K = 1: full FT (Ours) 75.90, LoRA 74.34, best PEFT AdaptFormer 74.55, linear probe 75.06, Spatial Adapter 76.25; full FT from self-supervised weights 4.39. K = 5: full FT 77.15, LoRA 76.30, Affine-LN 76.58, Spatial Adapter 76.78. K = 10: full FT 76.93, LoRA 77.79 (best), Spatial Adapter 77.35. K = 30: full FT 79.45, LoRA 80.04 (best), Spatial Adapter 78.44. Transductive inference adds +0.41 to +0.86 (Table 3; Spatial Adapter + TI at K = 10: 77.99).
- Results, novel structures, K = 10 (Table 4, p. 11): full FT (Ours) 52.38 avg; encoder-only PEFT 34.28–40.46 (LoRA 38.49); decoder fine-tuning 51.97; decoder FT + LoRA 57.39 (best, +5.0 over full FT); linear probe 8.68, Spatial Adapter 12.50.
- Results, FLARE'22 (Table 5, p. 12): zero-shot 73.70; at K = 30 full FT 78.62 vs LoRA 77.33 and Spatial Adapter 77.60, i.e. full fine-tuning best here (Sec. 5.2).
- Compute (Table 6; Sec. 5.3): full FT 62.1M trainable parameters; decoder 19.6M; PEFT about 5–7 min per TotalSegmentator task on one A6000 GPU at K = 5.
- Other: a randomly initialised head strongly degrades LoRA at all K (Fig. 6, Sec. 5.5).
- Limitations stated by the authors (Sec. 6, p. 16): novel organs need decoder fine-tuning, so parameter-efficient or black-box adaptation is compromised there; CT only; PEFT on purely convolutional U-Nets performs poorly. They note that the set of base organs is "expected to grow continually" (Sec. 6), without studying it.

## Relevance (NOT from the paper)
Defines few-shot as K labelled volumes with K = 1/5/10, as in this thesis, and shows that LoRA and full fine-tuning are close in that regime, with LoRA ahead at K ≥ 10 on known organs but encoder-only LoRA far behind on novel structures. It never measures forgetting or uses text prompts, so it supports the choice of shot counts and of LoRA as a strategy, not the continual-learning comparison.
