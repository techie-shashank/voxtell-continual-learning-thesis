## Facts (from the paper, each with location: Sec./p./Table/Fig.)
- Task / problem: free-text promptable 3D segmentation models are sensitive to wording; clinically equivalent rephrasings (abbreviations, synonyms, typos, clinical-order style) can change the mask substantially (Abstract; Sec. 1, pp. 1–2).
- Method (two sentences): a vision-language model (Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct, visual encoder frozen) reads multi-view 2D renderings of the volume and the free-text request, writes evidence, rationale and a canonical answer prompt, and the answer is passed to a frozen segmentation backbone (Sec. 2.2, Eq. 2; Sec. 3.1, p. 7). It is trained by SFT on SEER-Trace and then GRPO with Dice, stability-across-rephrasings and format rewards, maximising mean Dice minus λ × variance over a set of equivalent rephrasings (Eq. 3, p. 5).
- "Skill-evolving" (SEER-Loop, Sec. 2.3, pp. 5–6): a memory bank of text "skill artifacts" (e.g. abbreviation expansion) distilled from high-reward reasoning episodes, retrieved and appended to the VLM input, deduplicated and pruned by marginal reward gain (Eq. 4–6); run in discrete rounds, with the updated skills fed into the next training stage (p. 7). The segmentation backbone stays frozen; no sequence of segmentation tasks, no forgetting measurement. It is a growing reasoning memory, not continual learning of the segmentation model.
- Data (Sec. 2.1, p. 4): SEER-Trace, built from BraTS 2021, ISLES 2022, FLARE22, HVSMR-2.0, BraTS 2024 post-treatment glioma; 22,330 instruction instances from 1,811 cases. Requests generated by an LLM (DeepSeek-V3.2) in one of eight predefined clinical styles (e.g. radiology note, consult question), constrained by target aliases, visual priors and forbidden terms; expert audit of 1,120 sampled instances flagged 7 (0.63 %). Test: BrainMetShare (seen anatomy, unseen source) and PENGWIN pelvic bones (targets absent from SEER-Trace, "strictly OOD") (Sec. 3.1, p. 6). Number of test cases and rephrasings per case not stated.
- Prompt-sensitivity numbers (Table 1, p. 6; Dice in %; label prompting = native class-name interface; free-text = SEER-Trace-style prompts; Worst Dice and Std. over sets of equivalent rephrasings):
  - VoxTell: BrainMetShare label 48.19, free-text 52.15, worst 46.71, std 3.35; PENGWIN label 97.59, free-text 92.26, worst 79.34, std 7.49.
  - SAT: BrainMetShare 22.16 → 0.69 (worst 0.00, std 2.53); PENGWIN 96.05 → 0.01 (std 0.13).
  - BiomedParseV2: 18.66 → 2.53 (std 7.27); 1.35 → 8.53 (std 7.50). Text3DSAM: 0.10 → 0.41; 24.75 → 0.01. MedSAM3: 11.33 → 16.62 (worst 10.56, std 5.17); 18.26 → 5.75 (worst 3.67, std 6.40).
  - SEER: BrainMetShare label 51.70, free-text 53.83, worst 51.44, std 1.67; PENGWIN 97.56, 97.39, 95.47, 0.98.
- Ablation on PENGWIN (Table 2, p. 8): "Baseline" 92.26 / worst 79.34 / std 7.49; + vanilla VLM 84.84 / 61.90 / 14.15; + fine-tuned VLM with grounded reasoning 95.92 / 88.27 / 3.84; + SEER-Loop 97.39 / 95.47 / 0.98. INFERENCE (not stated in the paper, checked 2026-11-13): the Baseline row equals VoxTell's free-text row of Table 1, so the main SEER results most likely use VoxTell as the frozen backbone; the paper never names its default backbone (only the MedSAM3 swap, p. 8). Do not state it as a fact in the thesis.
- Headline: "reduces performance variance by 81.94 % and improves worst-case Dice by 18.60 %" (Abstract); how these two percentages are computed is not stated.
- Cross-backbone (p. 8): with MedSAM3 on PENGWIN, Dice 5.75 (std 6.40) → 19.97 (std 13.30), worst 3.67 → 3.94.
- Qualitative (Fig. 2, p. 7): three perturbation types shown: typo noise ("sacrun"), spatial specifier, clinical order.
- Limitations stated by the authors (Sec. 4, p. 9): only as future work: volumetric VLM encoders, larger skill taxonomy, broader clinical tasks, auditing failure modes.

## Where it fits in Ch. 2 (suggestion)
Section on free-text promptable 3D models (VoxTell and successors): independent evidence that VoxTell is the most robust of five text-promptable baselines to rephrasing but still loses up to 18 Dice points in the worst case (PENGWIN), supporting the thesis's need to state and fix its text prompts.

## Relevance (NOT from the paper)
Not continual learning in the thesis's sense (frozen backbone, no task sequence, no forgetting), so it does not affect the gap claim; it is the best available external number on VoxTell's prompt-wording sensitivity, though the rephrasings are LLM-generated and the test-set sizes are not given.
