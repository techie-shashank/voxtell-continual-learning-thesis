## Uncited claims in the range

- **l. 124** "Replay methods for vision--language models avoid storing real images by generating the replayed data." Uncited and too general. Liu et al. Sec. 4.1 also lists explicit replay with stored real samples (memory buffers, e.g. MSPT) and treats generation as one of two "implicit replay" directions (p. 12). Restrict to the methods reviewed ("The two replay methods reviewed here ...") or cite `liu2026continuallearningvlmssurvey` Sec. 4.1 for implicit replay.
- **l. 180** "All works in this section evaluate on image classification or on language tasks; none of them predicts a segmentation mask." It is the thesis's own summary, so no citation is needed, but as written it is not accurate. (1) LiLoRA (VQA, captioning, ImageNet instruction tasks) and MSLoRA-CR (VQA, report generation) work on vision–language tasks with text output, not "language tasks"; only O-LoRA is language-only. (2) "All works" includes the surveys and the metric papers. The Liu survey lists segmentation benchmarks (ADE20K-CL with IoU, Conceptual12M "Segmentation", Sec. 6 benchmark table), and Chaudhry et al. note that IoU for segmentation can replace accuracy. Hint: "All methods reviewed in this section are evaluated on image classification or on tasks with text output (text classification, VQA, captioning, report generation); none predicts a segmentation mask."
- l. 163 "learned prompt tokens, input vectors that are added to a frozen model". This is a definition and is acceptable uncited; Liu Sec. 4.3 "Prompt-Based Parameter Isolation" (p. 16) would cover it if wanted.
- l. 106, 112, 115, 119, 136, 160, 171: thesis structure or cross-references, no citation needed.
