1. **carion2026sam3segmentconcepts (intro l. 13)** is cited for a clinical text-prompting claim that SAM 3 does not make. SAM 3 is general-domain, accepts short noun phrases only and reports that it struggles with medical terms (App. B, p. 18).

3. **liu2026 (intro l. 27)** covers VLMs/MLLMs, not segmentation models. The transfer to promptable segmentation models is the thesis's own and should be worded as such.
4. **Close paraphrase.** Intro l. 22 copies the sentence pair and the citation chain of liu2026 p. 2 (regularisation [EWC], rehearsal [iCaRL], dynamic expansion [DEN]; "However, ... multimodal foundation models ... architectural ... scale"). Intro l. 27 follows liu2026 p. 2 ("early ... compact models ... in contrast, modern ... combine"). Intro l. 20 follows yu2026 p. 2 ("different modalities exhibit varying forgetting rates, further complicating"). The signed declaration requires marking or rewriting these.
5. **Bib, arXiv entry with a peer-reviewed venue stated in the PDF:** `yoon2018lifelonglearningdynamicallyexpandable`. The PDF states ICLR 2018.
6. **Bib, venue to check (not stated in the PDF):** `zou2023segment` (SEEM, likely NeurIPS 2023), `carion2026sam3segmentconcepts`, `cheng2023sammed2d`, `baharoon2025rexgroundingct3dchestct`, `liu2026continuallearningvlmssurvey`, `yu2026recentadvancesmultimodalcontinual`. All are @misc/arXiv, and their local PDFs name no venue.
7. Minor: "Early approaches" (intro l. 13) is not supported chronologically by MedSAM/SAM-Med2D. Degerli_2022 is the source of QaTa-COV19, but the text-annotated version used by TextMatch comes from LViT.
