## Uncited non-obvious claims

1. **l. 93** "To our knowledge, neither has been evaluated on 3D segmentation." Judgement: **the hedge is adequate in strength but vague, and it is not anchored to anything checkable.**
   - Nested learning: the claim is correct and understated. The NeurIPS version evaluates only language modelling and common-sense reasoning, and arXiv v1 adds text classification and translation. There is no image data in either version (nested_learning.md l. 10, 22).
   - MoE-Adapters: the original paper evaluates only 2D image classification with CLIP. MedCRP-CL, PA-EWC and MedPEFT-CL evaluated it as a baseline on **2D** CLIPSeg segmentation (Ch. 2 l. 245-250). A web search found no 3D use. Two MICCAI 2025 MoE segmentation papers (MoE-SAM; MoIE, CTTA) do not use or compare MoE-Adapters.
   - Risk: the Ch. 2 search (footnote, sec:sota-summary) was scoped to continual adaptation of 3D text-promptable models, not to every use of these two methods. "To our knowledge" therefore rests on an unstated search.
   - Hint: replace the negative existence claim with the verifiable positive one, e.g. "MoE-Adapters was evaluated on 2D classification and, as a baseline, on 2D segmentation \cite{gao2026medcrpcl,...}, and nested learning only on language tasks \cite{behrouz2025nestedlearning}." Alternatively, keep the hedge and add `(\cref{sec:sota-summary})`.
2. **l. 100** "Replaying such samples would avoid storing patient data." Uncited. The sentence is stronger than the sources. C²GR frames task-incremental learning "without access to previous task data" as "privacy-preserving" (abstract, p. 1; Sec. 1). C²GR does not claim that its generator holds no patient information, and the generator is trained on the patient data and kept across rounds. Hint: cite li2026c2gr for the framing and weaken the wording ("would avoid storing patient images, though the generator is trained on them"). Optionally name the cost: a second network trained every round, 2D only (Ch. 2 l. 189; notes l. 118).
3. **l. 110** "VoxTell checkpoint v1.0, which was not trained on AeroPath". Not cited here; it relies on `\cref{sec:data-aeropath}`, where data_description.tex l. 83 cites Suppl. Table 7 and the GitHub issue. Acceptable. Optional: repeat `\cite{rokuss2026voxtellissue15}`.
4. All other uncited sentences (l. 19-20, 22, 28, 32-75, 80-83, 89-91, 97, 105-109) report the thesis's own design or results, with sources given in the comments or by `\cref`, or they are future-work proposals. No further uncited factual claims about external work.
